How It Works

Essential Tournament Rules

Zero Tolerance
  • No Emulators: Mobile devices (iOS/Android) only. PCs or emulator software will be banned.
  • Score Proof: Captains must upload an uncropped screenshot of the end-game placement screen.
  • Punctuality: Custom room opens 15 minutes before match start. Lobby locks at exact match time.
  • Teaming & Exploits: Teaming between squads or hacking scripts results in instant team disqualification.
